FAQ

Straight answers
before you call.

By size, material, weight allowance, and rental length. Call with your address and load description for a flat, out-the-door quote before anything is scheduled.

Compact boxes suit single rooms and garage purges, mid-size covers remodels and full cleanouts, and large boxes suit whole-home projects. If you are between sizes, describe the pile on the call — sizing takes about a minute.

Hazardous waste, wet paint, tires, batteries, and similar restricted items are typically excluded everywhere. Mention appliances, Freon units, dirt, concrete, and shingles on the call so your load is routed correctly.

Driveway placements on private property generally do not. Street placement can require local approval — mention where you want the box and it will be confirmed when you book.

Agree on placement and day by phone, keep the drop zone clear for the truck, fill to the fill line, then call when full to arrange pickup. Have your preferred dates ready when you call.
